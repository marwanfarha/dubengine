dubengine

Two tracks of dub chords for the M VAVE FM1. The same code the FM1 runs, in your browser.

The FM1

Turn your phone sideways for bigger controls.

Knobs: turn with the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down turn faster. Buttons and keys: Space or Enter presses, held as long as you hold it; Shift with Space keeps a button held until you press it again. Keys: Left and Right arrows move between them.

Off. Tap Switch on.

Quick start

  1. Switch on, then press a lit key. Each one plays the chord the scale builds on it. Dark keys are outside the scale.
  2. HOME: KNOB 1 tempo, KNOB 2 root, KNOB 3 mode, KNOB 4 voicing.
  3. HOME again: the other track. The header shows A or B. Each track has its own key, pattern, sound, delay and EQ.
  4. SEL: metronome on or off.
  5. PLAY, then REC: play lit keys to record. REC again to stop recording.
  6. ENV, LFO, FX: shape the sound. Press again for the next page.
  7. GLO: levels and meters, then each track's EQ.

  • Tempo is shared. Root, Mode and Voicing belong to the selected track.
  • Delay: SELECT switches the time between Sync and Free. Feedback 100 holds forever, above 100 it swells and stays.
  • Delay 2: Mode is what a time change does (Repitch bends, Fade crossfades, Jump switches). Ping pong alternates left and right.
  • Reverb: one for both tracks. Mix is each track's own send.
  • EQ: 8 bands a track. Low cut, low shelf, four bells, high shelf, high cut.

Step edit

  1. Stop, then press REC. The 16 white keys are the steps.
  2. Longer than 16 steps: turn ALGORITHM for the next 16.
  3. Tap a step: add your last chord, or remove it.
  4. Hold a step and turn: KNOB 1 chord, KNOB 2 voicing, KNOB 3 octave, KNOB 4 length, ALGORITHM level, PRESETS cutoff, SELECT delay mix.
  5. REC again to leave.

Slots

  1. Hold SEQ (patterns) or ENV (sounds). The first 8 white keys are the slots. Lit keys have something in them.
  2. Tap a key: load that slot into the selected track.
  3. Hold a key for a second: save the selected track into that slot.

Randomise

  • EDIT on the Random page: a new pattern in the track's key. EDIT held: undo.
  • SAVE: nudge the sound. SAVE held: a whole new sound.
  • ARP: a new delay. ARP held: undo the last sound or delay change.
  • Nothing changes by itself. Each press is one roll.

The screen

  • Header: the page, the track, its key, play and record, the metronome, the tempo.
  • The picture shows what the page sets, to scale and live.
  • Footer: the four knobs. A bar shows where each sits in its range. The knob you turn lights orange.

Keyboard

Q to I and A to K: white keys. 1 to 0, -: black keys. Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, Z X OCT− OCT+. Drag knobs up and down, or scroll.

Saved settings stay in this browser. Factory reset forgets them.
